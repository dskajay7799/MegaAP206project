Returns
Items can be returned within 7 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging. Refunds go to the original payment method within 5 business days of inspection.

Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3 to 6 business days. Shipping is free on orders above Rs 999.

Warranty
Electronics carry a 12-month manufacturer warranty. Apparel and footwear are covered only for manufacturing defects reported within 7 days.
